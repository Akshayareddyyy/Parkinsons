Research Limitations

Critical scientific considerations regarding current quantum hardware constraints and clinical translation.

01

Simulation-Based Quantum Execution

The quantum models are currently executed via classical state-vector simulators (default.qubit) rather than physical NISQ hardware with physical gate infidelities.

02

External Cohort Validation Required

While zero subject leakage was mathematically guaranteed across the 42 Oxford subjects, generalization to demographically diverse clinical cohorts requires prospective evaluation.

03

Research-Use Only

This platform is an academic comparative methodology benchmark. It is not approved by regulatory medical agencies and does not constitute a clinical diagnostic tool.
