About the Research

Investigation of non-invasive vocal biomarkers for motor impairment stratification without patient-level data leakage.

🎯

Clinical Problem

Parkinson's Disease (PD) motor symptoms progressively deteriorate vocal cord control, inducing micro-fluctuations in pitch, frequency, and harmonic structure. Traditional clinical assessment requires in-clinic evaluation of the Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (motor UPDRS). This study automates severity stratification via non-invasive telemonitoring into mild (Class 0) versus moderate-to-severe (Class 1) impairment.

📊

Oxford Telemonitoring Cohort

Comprises 5,875 biomedical voice recordings collected over 6 months from 42 individuals with early-stage Parkinson's disease. Phonation signals capture sustained /a/ vowels digitized under telemonitoring protocols, yielding high-dimensional acoustic indicators alongside clinical UPDRS ratings and patient demographics.

🔒

Subject-Wise Zero-Leakage Protocol

A critical pitfall in medical machine learning is random sample-wise splitting, which places recordings from the same individual into both training and testing partitions, causing inflated performance through identity leakage. To guarantee absolute clinical validity, all subjects are strictly partitioned at the patient level:

Development Cohort 26 Subjects 3,602 Recordings (61.3%) · Feature selection & local client training
Validation Cohort 7 Subjects 983 Recordings (16.7%) · Hyperparameter tuning & early stopping
Locked Test Cohort 9 Subjects 1,290 Recordings (22.0%) · Completely unseen final evaluation

ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN

Methodology

Systematic progression across Centralized, Federated, and Variational Quantum Neural Network architectures.

PARADIGM 01

Centralized Learning

Aggregates all training recordings into a single server. Evaluates regularized baselines (Logistic Regression and Extremely Randomized Trees) to establish upper-bound classical benchmarks.

→
PARADIGM 02

Federated Learning

Simulates 26 decentralized edge clients corresponding to individual development subjects. Clients compute local tree splits and model parameters without transmitting raw audio telemetry.

→
PARADIGM 03

Quantum Machine Learning

Projects 12 standardized features onto 4 qubits in a $2^4 = 16$-dimensional complex Hilbert space via $RY/RZ$ angle rotations, entangling CNOT ring topology, and variational Pauli-$Z$ observable measurement.
